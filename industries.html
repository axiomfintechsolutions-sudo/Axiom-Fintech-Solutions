Manufacturing & Industrial

Manufacturing businesses operate on tight margins with complex cost structures — raw materials, labour, overheads, wastage, and yield all affecting profitability in ways that are hard to track without the right systems. We build the financial infrastructure that gives you complete visibility.

From cost-of-production reporting to variance analysis against standard costs, our work helps factory owners and plant managers across the US, UK, and GCC understand exactly where money is being made — and where it's being lost.

Common Challenges We Solve
No visibility into true cost-per-unit across product lines
Manual production reporting — slow, error-prone, and outdated
Inconsistent bank loan applications with weak financial documentation
Inability to forecast cash flow during seasonal demand swings

Technology & SaaS

Tech founders move fast — but investors look at the numbers. If your MRR, ARR, churn, LTV, and CAC aren't clearly structured and IFRS-aligned, fundraising becomes an uphill battle. We build the financial infrastructure that makes your startup investor-ready.

From deferred revenue schedules and segment reporting to full investor data rooms, we ensure your financial story is as compelling as your product story — structured, clean, and built to withstand due diligence.

Common Challenges We Solve
Mixed cash and accrual records — not IFRS compliant for investors
No structured SaaS metrics — MRR, ARR, churn, LTV not tracked
Investors requesting financials you don't have in the right format
No segment reporting for multiple product lines or revenue streams

We build the financial systems that bring complete clarity to your revenue cycle and operational costs.
